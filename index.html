ModelAudit

Individual capstone · 2026
Live research preview.

An evidence-grounded tool for appraising studies that develop clinical prediction models. Upload a PDF and it retrieves relevant passages, proposes structured findings against selected TRIPOD+AI and PROBAST+AI criteria, and links each finding to quoted evidence for a human reviewer to check. I designed and built it end to end.

Pipeline
PDF text extraction, BM25 and semantic retrieval combined with Reciprocal Rank Fusion, LLM-assisted assessment per item, and deterministic rules for TRIPOD+AI item categories rather than leaving them to the model.
Scope and limits
Covers 14 selected TRIPOD+AI items and 8 PROBAST+AI questions, not either framework in full, and gives no overall risk-of-bias judgement. Quotation checks confirm the text exists in the paper, not that it supports the finding.
Engineering
FastAPI backend in Docker on Azure Container Apps, built with GitHub Actions. React and TypeScript frontend on Vercel.

HyperSense

Independent project · Full technical ownership
2026 · Deployed prototype (v1.1).

An explainable machine learning screener for hypertension risk in West Africa. From non-invasive inputs (age, sex, residence, education, tobacco use, height and weight), the app returns a High or Low risk tier, a probability, a SHAP explanation of the factors behind it, and a preventive recommendation. It is a screening tool, not a diagnostic one. I built the data harmonisation, modelling, explainability and deployment, then tested whether the model holds up in Nigeria.

Model
XGBoost on Ghana DHS 2014 (7,844 adults), survey-weighted, labelled from measured blood pressure only. ROC-AUC about 0.79, with the threshold chosen on out-of-fold predictions to avoid test-set leakage.
Nigerian external validation
Applied unchanged to 4,089 Nigerian adults, AUC fell from 0.792 to 0.747 and specificity from 0.616 to 0.448, with marked overprediction.
Regional adaptation
Retraining on a pooled Ghana–Nigeria cohort (11,933 adults) gave AUC 0.803, sensitivity 0.759, specificity 0.698 and near-ideal calibration. Adding 12 clinical predictors improved discrimination only modestly.

Potato Leaf Detection & Disease Classification System

Collaborative project · Backend engineering
2026 · Deployed prototype.

A two-stage computer-vision app. A detector first checks that an uploaded image is a potato leaf, then a classifier labels it healthy, early blight or late blight, with a confidence score and a plain-language explanation. The detector gates the input so the classifier does not force a disease label onto a cup or a tomato leaf.

My contributionBuilt the FastAPI backend: image-upload validation, the sequential detector-then-classifier pipeline and structured prediction responses. I also contributed to dataset work. Model training and the frontend were part of the wider team effort.

Held-out results
The classifier got all 323 held-out images right, but only 152 of 2,152 images were healthy, so treat this as a sanity check, not field performance. The detector reached 97.01% on 234 held-out images, accepting 7 of 127 non-potato images.
